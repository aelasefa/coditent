## Crop guidance for the later integration
Square is the native and preferred framing. Centered 3:4 portrait crops preserve both full people; peripheral foliage may crop. 4:3 landscape crops with object-position: 50% 80% preserve people and feet but trim the tops of arches/tree canopy. Do not use arbitrary centered wide crops or 1.45 scaling. Use square/portrait or contain when the entire architectural/botanical silhouette must remain visible. No universal crop safety is claimed for very wide or very narrow frames.
Quiet cream space surrounds the Sign In vignette and occupies the upper/right areas in Sign Up; keep interface text in HTML outside the image.
